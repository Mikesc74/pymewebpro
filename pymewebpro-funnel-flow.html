What changes vs. today

  • NEW Awaiting reply. Today, the moment Santi sends the first message the lead disappears from the columns until the next day. This state keeps it visible, with no action asked, so nothing falls through.
  • NEW In conversation. Today there is no "they replied, we are talking" state. That is where deals close. Santi marks it with one tap when the prospect replies.
  • "Follow-ups" → "Needs a nudge". Only shows the ones that actually went cold (cadence due), not everything that was sent.
  • The mockup is a signal, not a stage. It can be sent from several points; opening it fires the alert and raises priority.

Decisions to lock (forks)

1. Four working columns (To contact · In conversation · Needs a nudge · To close) with "Awaiting reply" as a collapsed list below? Or keep three and fold "Awaiting reply" into follow-ups?
2. Is "Did they reply?" a manual one-tap by Santi, or do we try to detect the reply another way? (Human-sent WhatsApp gives no read receipt, so a manual tap is the only honest option today.)
3. Can the mockup be sent from "Awaiting reply" and "In conversation," or only once they have replied?
4. DB mapping, no migration needed: lead_stage=contacted = In conversation, sales_qualified = To close, status=converted = Won, disqualified = Lost. Agree?

The gap today, concretely

When Santi taps "Open WhatsApp and log," the system only saves last_touched_at and bumps the touch count. It never changes lead_stage. The follow-up cadence starts at D+1. Result: the lead leaves "To contact" (it now has a touch) but is not yet in "Follow-ups" (not due), so it goes invisible until tomorrow. The Awaiting reply state covers that gap.
