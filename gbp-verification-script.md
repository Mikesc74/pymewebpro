# PymeWebPro · GBP video verification, shot-list script

One continuous take. No cuts, no stop-start. About 2 to 3 minutes. Film horizontal. Narrate as you go (English or Spanish, Google doesn't care). If you fumble, just restart the whole take.

## Before you hit record (lay this out first)

On the guest-room desk, have ready and visible:

- Laptop open, screen on, with these tabs already loaded:
  - pymewebpro.com
  - one client mockup (mockups.pymewebpro.com/<slug>/)
  - the Google Business Profile dashboard logged in (or your business email inbox)
- A printed business document with the company name: Cámara de Comercio certificate or RUT for **Norte Sur Consulting S.A.S.**
- A business card if you have one

Do one dry run of the walk without recording so the route is smooth. Good lighting on, lens wiped.

Do NOT film: your cédula, bank details, or any full ID/financial numbers. The company registration (Cámara / RUT) is fine.

## The take (record this, one shot, top to bottom)

1. **Outside the building.** Stand at the entrance. Slowly pan to show the street, the building name or number, and a nearby landmark or street sign.
   > "Hi, I'm Mike Chartrand, owner of PymeWebPro. This is our building here in El Poblado, Medellín."

2. **Into the lobby, to the elevator.** Keep filming. Step in, press **8**, and show the floor indicator (the lit "8" or the numbers climbing).
   > "We work from my home office on the eighth floor."

3. **Out on the 8th floor, to your door.** Walk to the apartment. You can briefly show the unit number.

4. **Inside, through to the guest room.** Keep walking in the same shot into the office.
   > "This is my home office, the guest room I work from every day."

5. **At the desk, show the work on screen.** Point the camera at the laptop and click through:
   - pymewebpro.com
   - the client mockup
   - the GBP dashboard or your business email
   > "This is the PymeWebPro site I build and run, a live client page, and our Google Business Profile."

6. **Show the company document.** Hold up the Cámara de Comercio certificate or RUT (and a business card).
   > "And this is our company registration, Norte Sur Consulting S.A.S., the legal entity behind PymeWebPro."

7. **Stop recording.**

## After

- Upload the same take Google asks for. You usually get a few attempts.
- Review can take anywhere from a few minutes to about 5 business days.
- If it's rejected, the most common fix is making step 1 (location) and step 6 (ownership document) clearer and longer. Lean on those two for a home-based digital business.
