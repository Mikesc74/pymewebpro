9DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Sell less, ask more. The prospect should describe the problem before you describe the fix.

Lead with questions, then stay quiet and let them talk. Their own words become your pitch.

Situation where they are now
  • How do customers usually find you?
  • What does your website actually do for you today?
  • Who answers when a customer calls or messages?
Pain what's leaking
  • What happens when a call goes unanswered?
  • How many enquiries come in after hours or on weekends?
  • When you're out on a job, who's catching the next lead?
Impact what it costs them
  • What's one new customer worth to you?
  • If you booked a few more jobs a month, what would that change?
  • How much time do you lose answering the same questions?
Listen for the number. The moment they tell you what a customer is worth, you've found the line you'll close on later.

Once they've named the problem, bridge straight to the demo: "Let me show you exactly how we'd fix that."
