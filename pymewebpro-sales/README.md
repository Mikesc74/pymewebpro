# PymeWebPro · Sales System

Operational sales infrastructure for a productized funnel studio. This is not a generic agency kit. Every piece exists to help commission salespeople close fast while keeping scope fixed, expectations controlled, and operations clean.

## What we sell

ONE standardized product plus ONE optional monthly plan. Fixed scope, fixed price, fast launch. No tiers, no à la carte add-ons.

| Producto | Price | Includes | Launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **La página de ventas** | $400.000 COP one-time (IVA incl.) | Ficha de Google configurada + first month of the monthly plan | ~48 horas |
| **Plan mensual** (optional) | $150.000 COP/mes (IVA incl.) | Todo incluido (see below) | after the included first month |

The monthly plan is all-inclusive: hosting + Ficha de Google activa y actualizada + versión bilingüe ES/EN + soporte WhatsApp + hasta 2 cambios/mes + asistente de ventas 24/7 que conoce el negocio, responde y agenda llamadas + vitrina de hasta 30 productos + catálogo o menú descargable + reporte mensual. No contract, cancel anytime. Everything lives inside this one plan, never a separate line item.

Payment split: 30% deposit ($120.000) to start, 70% ($280.000) on launch. Wompi only, COP only.

Anything not in the product or the plan is out of scope by default and requires approval. A real store and a blog are out of scope but quotable as custom. See `internal/scope-boundaries.md`.

**Retired:** the old Essential $390.000 / Pro $690.000 tiers, the $50.000/mes Plan de presencia, the $30.000/mes hosting-only tier, and ALL à la carte add-ons are gone. The chatbot (now the asistente 24/7), bilingual version, showcase, and catalog are inside the $150.000/mes plan.

## The files

Client-facing (Spanish):

- **`one-pager.html`** · the sales one-pager. Open in any browser. Send to a prospect or present on a call.
- **`configurador.html`** · the "Resumen de la oferta" quote page. Shows the single product ($400.000), the 30/70 split, the optional Plan mensual ($150.000/mes), a scope guard, and a copy-paste WhatsApp quote. No add-on engine: there is one product and one optional plan.
- **`guion-ventas.md`** · the WhatsApp sales script framework. Opener, discovery, positioning, objections, close.

Internal (English, do not send to clients):

- **`internal/add-on-menu.md`** · RETIRED. There are no à la carte add-ons. The file now documents the single-bundle model (one $400.000 product + one $150.000/mes plan).
- **`internal/scope-boundaries.md`** · what is included, not included, add-on-only, and completely outside our model. Operationally critical.
- **`internal/sales-rulebook.md`** · what reps can and cannot promise, what needs approval, how to position, how to avoid scope creep.
- **`internal/client-qualification.md`** · ideal clients, red flags, disqualifying project types, signals a client will get expensive.
- **`internal/visual-style-direction.md`** · the design system and the look-and-feel rules behind the collateral.
- **`internal/production-reconciliation.md`** · the guide is the source of truth; this is the exact file-by-file punch-list to bring the live site and Valentina in line with it. Read before touching production.

Hosting / deploy:

- **`index.html`** · the rep hub for `ventas.pymewebpro.com` (Access-gated). Links the configurator, the PDF download, and the manual.
- **`one-pager-print.html`** · the print source for the PDF. Edit this, then regenerate the PDF (see `DEPLOY.md`).
- **`PymeWebPro-Funnels.pdf`** · the client leave-behind. Two pages, downloadable from the hub or sent on WhatsApp.
- **`build-site.py`** · assembles the deployable `dist/` (copies rep files, converts the rep docs to HTML, excludes the Mike/Santi-only docs).
- **`DEPLOY.md`** · step-by-step to deploy to `ventas.pymewebpro.com` (Cloudflare Pages + custom domain + Access).
- **`dist/`** · generated output of `build-site.py`. Do not edit by hand; it is rebuilt each run.

## How a deal runs

1. Qualify the prospect against `internal/client-qualification.md`. Disqualify early. A bad-fit deal costs more than it pays.
2. Open and discover using `guion-ventas.md`.
3. Quote with `configurador.html` (Resumen de la oferta). Never quote a custom number from your head.
4. Send the one-pager (`one-pager.html`) plus the offer summary.
5. Stay inside `internal/scope-boundaries.md` and `internal/sales-rulebook.md`. Anything outside requires approval before you promise it.
6. Close on the standard 30/70 Wompi terms.

## House rules baked into everything here

- **Colombia only, COP only.** No USD, no CAD, no mixed currency. Ever.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, " · ".
- **Fixed scope is the product.** We sell a system, not hours. If a rep is improvising scope, the system has failed.
- **Honest framing.** Real numbers, no fabricated case studies, no "world-class" filler.

## Deployment note

This folder is intentionally kept out of the public Cloudflare Pages repos so the internal docs are not web-exposed. If you want the one-pager or configurator online, deploy them behind Cloudflare Access (Mike + Santi + reps), not on the public site.
