There is ONE product and ONE optional monthly plan. No tiers, no à la carte add-ons.

**La página de ventas · $400.000 COP, IVA incluido.** A high-conversion sales funnel, custom built (no templates), live in ~48 hours. Includes:

- Custom conversion-focused sales page, one primary CTA
- 100% responsive (phone, tablet, desktop)
- WhatsApp button or contact form, click-to-call button
- Google Maps embed
- Booking/appointment integration (Cal.com, Calendly)
- Testimonials section
- SEO structure (schema, meta, sitemap)
- Privacy-first analytics (Cloudflare)

- **Ficha de Google (Google Business Profile) configurada** (now in the base, not an add-on)
- **First month of the monthly plan included**
- 2 revision rounds
- Live in ~48 hours

Payment: 30/70 split. 30% deposit = **$120.000 COP** to start, 70% = **$280.000 COP** at launch. Wompi (tarjetas, PSE, transferencia), COP only.

## Out of scope but QUOTABLE (custom quote, never standard)

A few common asks are NOT part of the product and NOT in the plan. They get a separate scope and price from Mike or Santi:

- **Real store / ecommerce** (cart, checkout, inventory, variants, shipping). The vitrina de hasta 30 productos in the plan is the productized stopping point; a real store is a custom quote.
- **Blog** (a CMS, article templates, ongoing publishing). A real blog is a custom quote.

## What is NEVER quoted, even custom, without approval

Custom web apps, client portals, membership/login systems, mobile apps, advanced/multi-step marketing automations, ongoing ad management, SEO retainers, rebranding/logo design, and anything described as "just a small custom feature." All outside the model. See `scope-boundaries.md`.
