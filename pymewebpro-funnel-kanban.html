How cards move

  • To contact → Follow-up: Santi sends the first message, card moves on its own. (auto)
  • Inside Follow-up: when a nudge is due (D+1 / D+3 / D+7) the card turns red, jumps to top, ⚠ Follow up now. Santi drafts a follow-up (resets to waiting) or, if they replied well, taps "Replied → advance."
  • Follow-up → Mockup: Santi advances a positive reply. (tap) On arrival the system builds the named mockup + the WhatsApp message (auto), then turns red ⚠ Ready to send. Santi reviews and sends.
  • Mockup → Proposal: after a good mockup reply, Santi advances. (tap) He ticks what the client wants (add-ons + notes). The system writes the proposal in the background (auto), then turns red ⚠ Ready to send.
  • Proposal → To close: Santi sends the proposal, card moves on its own. (auto)
  • Inside To close: the system watches for movement. If the deal goes quiet (no open, no reply) for a few days, it drafts a nudge to close and turns the card red ⚠ Nudge ready. (auto) Santi reviews and sends. The card also turns red the moment they open the proposal or the deposit lands.
  • To close → Closed: Won (deposit paid) or Lost. Stale leads auto-flag for Lost after 14 days.
  • Lost from anywhere: at any stage, if the prospect says no, Santi drags the card into the Closed column. It leaves the board and can be revived later. (drag)
