Frequently asked questions

How smart is the assistant?

It runs on Claude Sonnet 4.6, the model behind Anthropic's flagship product. We train it on your services, prices, hours, and FAQ from your form, so it knows your business specifically. When it doesn't know an answer, it hands you the conversation instead of guessing.

Does it speak English?

Yes. It detects Spanish or English and replies in the same language. The bilingual version is included in the monthly plan.

Do you build online stores or membership sites?

Our package is built to win customers for service businesses, but if you have a different need we're happy to take a look and put a proposal together. Open the chat and tell us what you have in mind.

Why does it cost less than an agency?

Because it's one focused product, not an agency with project managers and hourly billing. We build it efficiently, with automation where we can, and pass you one clear fixed price, everything included, no surprise charges.

What if I want to cancel?

It's month to month. Email us with 30 days notice. You keep your domain, and if you want them, we hand off your site files.

What if the assistant says something wrong to my customer?

It's trained only on your business and answers from what you give us. When it isn't sure, it hands you the conversation instead of guessing, and flags anything sensitive. You approve what it knows before it goes live, and you can change it anytime.

Is the page mine, or am I locked in?

It's yours. You keep your domain, it's month to month, and if you ever leave we hand off your site files. No lock-in, no fine print.

Isn't a single page too thin for a real business?

A focused page usually converts better than one full of tabs, because it points every visitor at one action: contacting you. If you need more pages, a store, or a blog, we add them. Most businesses get more customers from one clear page than from ten they never update.
